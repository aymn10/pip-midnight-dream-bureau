# Submission preparation

## Title
Pip’s Midnight Dream Bureau

## Track
Track 1 — Pip, the Skull Baby

## Description
Pip’s Midnight Dream Bureau is a tiny world for a tiny dream engineer. Visitors meet an original illustrated skull baby, discover how a forgotten music box became a midnight workshop, and make themselves at home among sleeping clouds and softly lit rooftops.

Everything in the workshop is playable: wind an original synthesized lullaby, tune a nightlight’s glow, follow deliveries around an imaginary town, or turn the pages of a bedtime story. The nursery rewards curiosity, and three numbered stars unlock a secret dream where the moon becomes a boat for two.

The visual language mixes early-web keepsakes with a carefully composed storybook: constellation clues, a local visit counter, paper notes, original SVG illustrations and a sparkle cursor. All sound respects one visible mute switch. Reduced-motion preferences and mobile layouts are built in. Wishes save locally, and the interface clearly says so.
